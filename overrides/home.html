How a layer is made

  1. Move Z

    The build plate steps to the next layer height, measured from the vat film.

  2. Expose

    The projector shines the layer's mask up through the film and cures it.

  3. Write silver

    On layers with conductive features, the galvo steers the 405 nm laser along each line and reduces the silver salt in the resin.

  4. Peel

    The plate lifts in two stages to free the layer from the film, then comes back down for the next one.

Specifications

60.0 by 37.5 mm, 1280 by 800 projector pixels
Projector pixel
46.85 µm at the build plane
Projector
TI DLP LightCrafter 4500, blue LED
Laser
405 nm diode on a two-axis galvo scanner
Z resolution
1.25 µm per microstep, homed to an optical switch
Laser placement
About 160 µm expected error after alignment
Control
Web interface on the host laptop; Arduino Mega 2560 controller
